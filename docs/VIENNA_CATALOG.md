# Vienna launch catalogue

`202609290002_vienna_manual_catalog.sql` seeds 24 venues selected for variety
across price points, cuisines and districts. It deliberately omits ratings,
reviews, provider photos and opening hours: these change often and should not
be invented or copied from Google.

The venue names and street addresses were checked against TIAN's Vienna
recommendations page on 29 September 2026. Each record has a venue website so
the operations team can verify it before publishing and maintain it directly.

## Publishing checklist

- Call or email each venue to confirm its listing, website and desired tags.
- Verify coordinates and address from the restaurant's own contact page.
- Use only images you own, license, create, or receive permission to use.
- Set `source_updated_at` after each editorial review.
- Remove a venue promptly if it closes or asks not to be listed.
